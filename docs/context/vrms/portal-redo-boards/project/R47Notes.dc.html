Round 47: S27, plain

Picked: S27 as it stood, the map under the whole page and the form on navy glass. No garage turn-taking (that was S28), and no traffic dots on the expressways. The twelve trucks still drive their runs out and back.

WHAT CHANGES FROM BOARD TO BOARD

  • The form's surface: S27's glass (as picked), heavier frost, a floating card, or no surface at all.
  • The map's look: as picked, dimmed back, gold etching, or the city lit up.
  • The angle: tilted (every board but one) or flat.

One surface and one map look can go together, for example the floating card on the etched map.

FOR THE BUILD

  • Blur over moving trucks costs a little. The glass re-blurs whatever moves under it. The panel and the card blur only a few trucks; no surface (Open) blurs nothing.
  • Reduced motion parks the trucks; the glass then never re-blurs.
  • The map credit stays in the footer under the form, as on Round 46.
