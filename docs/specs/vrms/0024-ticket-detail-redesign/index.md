# 0024. Ticket detail page around foldable issue rows

**Date**: 2026-11-06
**Status**: In Progress

## Summary

The repair ticket page is rebuilt around the work. Each issue becomes a row that folds open and closed (an accordion), showing its progress and state when closed, so a ticket with 10 issues and up to 20 steps each stays readable. Reference clutter goes (typical parts and hours), photos get their own card after the issues with one add control that says where the photo lands, AI and Activity fold to one line, and no part of the page is joined by a dot, the title follows the design file, Cancel ticket moves into a menu, and on phones the issues come before people and details. It is mostly a web change on data the page already has. Task 8 adds one optional field to the photo upload and narrows Before photo protection on the server (no new endpoint, no migration). Task 9 turns the Status card into label and value rows, gives both menus real items, adds a late Before photo tile with a warning notice, and restyles every pill in the app. Task 10 moves each row's progress onto its second line on phones, because the fixed progress column left the title only about 60px wide at 375px.

## Requirements

**User stories**:
- As a mechanic on my phone, I want the issue I am working on open and the rest folded, so that I tick steps without scrolling past finished work.
- As a verifier, I want every issue folded with what fixed it on the row, so that I can sign off a whole ticket from one screen.
- As an Admin, I want the page to look like a well made work order, plain where it can be and coloured only where it means something, so that the important things stand out.
- As anyone, I want the plate and the main issue as the title, so that I know which truck I am looking at.

**Acceptance criteria**:

Issue rows
- **AC-1**: Issues render as one list of foldable rows (shadcn Accordion, `type="multiple"`), split by thin dividers inside the Issues card, with no bordered box inside a row. The whole row header is one button (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, at least 44px tall on phones), inside an `h3`, and folding uses the `ease-snappy` curve (none with reduced motion).
- **AC-2**: A closed row is at most two lines (on phones the second line may wrap, see the end of this item). Line 1: the issue number, the issue text (semibold, one step above body text: `text-base` from 768px, `text-sm` on phones) (2 lines at most on phones, 1 from 768px, cut with an ellipsis), and on the right the progress (from 768px; on phones the progress moves to line 2, AC-3) and the chevron. Line 2, muted `text-xs`, its parts split by space only (`gap-3`, never a dot or other symbol): the category, then "Known fix: {name}" or "No known fix", then the word "Safety" in the warning colour when the linked known fix has safety notes (trimmed, not empty). The known fix name is plain text here (never a link: the header is one button with no controls inside), with its status badge when the entry is Draft or Inactive. Once the issue has a saved resolution, a closed row's line 2 shows "What fixed it" instead (an open row keeps the category, known fix and Safety line, since the resolution block shows below it, AC-7): the note, else "Followed the known fix" when `followedKnownFix`, else nothing, cut to 1 line, then the hours as "2.5 h" when `hoursSpent` is set, split from the note by space. A Done issue with no saved resolution while the ticket is In progress shows "Needs what fixed it" in muted text at the end of line 2. Once a resolution is saved, line 2 holds only What fixed it and the hours (no category, known fix or Safety word); the safety line inside the open row (AC-8) still shows. "Needs what fixed it" shows only while the ticket is In progress, never in On hold, Outsourced, Pending approval or later. Below 768px line 2 holds the progress label first (AC-3), then these parts, in every variant (the closed What fixed it line, the open category line and the plain line), and it may wrap onto a second line (`flex-wrap`, `gap-x-3 gap-y-0.5`, `items-center`) so no part is ever cut down to nothing: each part moves down whole, and a part that alone is wider than the line (the note, the known fix name, a long category) ends in an ellipsis (`max-w-full min-w-0 truncate`; on the known fix the `max-w-full` sits on its wrapper, so a Draft or Inactive badge stays whole and `shrink-0`). The Returned pill makes that line 20px tall instead of 16px, which is accepted. From 768px line 2 is one line as above (`md:flex-nowrap md:gap-3 md:overflow-hidden md:whitespace-nowrap`). An open row shows the whole title at every width; the one and two line cuts above apply to a closed row only.
- **AC-3**: The issue's state (derived, nothing stored) shows as the progress text with a slim bar under it (4px, full width of the progress column from 768px; on phones the bar sits under line 2, see below):
  - **Returned**: the issue has a saved resolution but no `resolvedAt` (spec 0014); gold `highlight` text "Returned", bar hidden. Wins over every other state.
  - **No checklist**: the issue has no steps; muted text, no bar.
  - **Not started**: steps exist, none ticked; muted "Not started", empty bar.
  - **In progress**: some ticked; "{x} of {y}" in tabular figures, bar in the In progress token.
  - **Done**: all ticked; "Done" in the `success` colour, full bar in `success`.
  From 768px the progress column has a fixed width (`w-24`, inside the fold button, before the row menu), its text right aligned and its bar the column's full width; every state reserves the width, No checklist and Returned included, so the state words line up on every row.
  Below 768px there is no progress column. A checked build at 375px showed why: the card padding, the two 44px targets (the row menu and the chevron) and a 96px column left the title column about 62px wide, so a title wrapped one word per line and line 2 collapsed to nothing. On phones the progress label is the first part of line 2 instead, in the same words and colours (the Returned pill, No checklist, Not started, "{x} of {y}", Done), as just the label with none of the column's classes (`shrink-0`, no `w-24`, no right alignment). Its 4px bar is a separate phone only strip (`md:hidden`, `mt-1 h-1 w-full`) placed after the whole of line 2 in the text column, so it sits under the line even when line 2 wraps; it is hidden for Returned and No checklist as before and takes no height then. The title column then gets about 170px at 375px. This holds for open and closed rows alike, and in each of line 2's variants (AC-2). One placement shows at a time at any width (the other is `display: none`, so the row button's name reads the progress once, with a space between its parts). Nothing changes from 768px.
  Ticks made on this page count at once: the local tick overrides move up from `IssueBlock` into one `useTickOverrides` hook for the whole list (keyed by step id, cleared for an issue when its `checklistVersion` changes, and an override dropped once the server value equals it), so the closed row, the summary line and the open rule all read the same merged steps (`mergedSteps` in `lib/issueRow.ts`). The overrides live outside the folding content, so folding a row never drops a pending tick or its rollback. Returned rests on the rule that resolution notes are saved only on the move to For verification, so notes without `resolvedAt` mean the ticket came back.
- **AC-4**: Which rows start open, on a first visit to this ticket in this tab: while the ticket is Open, Assigned, In progress, On hold, Outsourced or Pending approval (an allow list in `initialOpen`, not "every other status"), the first row that is not Done and not No checklist opens (a Returned row counts as not Done), and every row opens when the ticket has 3 issues or fewer; in For verification, Closed and Cancelled every row starts closed. The rule depends on status only, not role. Issue order is the array order the ticket response sends (by `createdAt`, then `id`; a backend test locks it). A link with `#issue-N` (N from 1, today's order) opens that row on top of whatever is open or stored, and scrolls it into view once per hash value (rows carry `scroll-margin-top` for the sticky header); an N out of range is ignored.
- **AC-5**: Open and closed rows are remembered per ticket in `sessionStorage` (`vrms.ticket.<id>.view`, shape `{ open: string[] }`, checked when read, at most the 20 most recent tickets kept). The open value lives in `useTicketView`, never derived from props, and drives the Accordion as a controlled `value`. A stored state wins over AC-4 on a reload. The 3 second refresh never opens or closes a row; an issue that appears later starts closed; a removed issue drops out of the stored state. If storage fails, the page uses AC-4 and keeps state in memory. An older stored shape still reads: its `open` list is kept when valid, and any `hideDone` field (boolean, list or anything else) is ignored and dropped on the next write. The 20 ticket prune stays.
- **AC-6**: Above the rows, one line: "{d} of {n} issues done, {t} of {s} steps ticked" (the step part counts only issues with steps and is left out when none have steps; "1 issue" and "1 step" in the singular). The line has no controls: no Hide done steps switch, no Expand all or Collapse all. Each row's header opens and closes it, and the open rows are remembered (AC-5).

Inside an open row
- **AC-7**: Top to bottom: the safety note, the checklist, one quiet "Edit steps" (or "Add steps") text button, then the resolution. Shown read only, the resolution is one block: a small "What fixed it" label with the hours ("3.5 h") on the right of that line only when `hoursSpent` is set, then the note (or "Followed the known fix") under it; no separate "Hours spent" field and no "—". The issue's other actions sit in a `⋮` row menu (`dropdown-menu`, 44px target on phones) on the row header line, between the progress column and the chevron, shown whether the row is open or closed: Edit issue and Remove issue (spec 0019, Remove in `destructive` text), Change known fix (relink, spec 0007, only when the issue may be relinked) and Open known fix (`/knowledge-base/<entryId>`, only while `knowledgeOn`). Each keeps its current `can` rule; the `⋮` is not shown when the menu would be empty. Choosing Change known fix shows the link picker inline in the row with "Remove link" and "Keep it". Edit issue and Remove issue follow `canEditDetails` (the edit flag and `ticketEditable`, as today); Remove is hidden when the ticket has one issue (spec 0019, AC-17). The menu trigger is a sibling of the fold button inside the row header, never inside it (the header stays one button with no controls inside): `AccordionTrigger` gets an `actions` slot rendered inside the Radix `Header` after the button, and the row's chevron moves out of the button to sit after the actions (`aria-hidden`, clicking it toggles the row, like `FoldCard`'s); and it is named "More actions for issue {n}" and shows a "More actions" tooltip on hover and focus. The menu is the shared `MoreMenu` (the Status card uses it too), which keeps that tooltip from opening again when focus returns to the button after the menu closes. Every item carries a small icon (a pencil for Edit issue, a link for Change known fix, an open book for Open known fix, a bin for Remove issue), a divider sits before Remove issue whenever another item shows, the menu is at least 176px wide (`min-w-44`), and Open known fix is menu text colour, not link blue.
- **AC-8**: The safety note is one line of `warning` tinted text (no box, no icon) above the checklist, cut to 1 line (`line-clamp-1`) with "More" shown only when the text is actually cut off, expanding it in place. It never hides behind a fold other than the row itself, and the closed row's "Safety" word (AC-2) points to it.
- **AC-9**: The checklist keeps its ticks, rules and tags (specs 0007, 0021, 0023). Done steps show dimmed (no strikethrough) and always stay in the list; nothing hides them. Step rows are at least 44px tall on phones and 36px from 768px.
- **AC-10**: Typical parts and typical hours are no longer shown on the ticket, and the open row has no separate known fix line: the name is on line 2 of the row header (AC-2) with its status badge when Draft or Inactive, and Open known fix and Change known fix live in the row menu (AC-7). An issue with no known fix keeps the link picker (when it may be linked) and "Suggest a known fix" inline in the open row.

Page
- **AC-11**: The title is the plate chip, then the model when `vehicleModel` is set, then the first issue's text (cut to 1 line; left out when the ticket has no issues); the ticket ID sits below in `muted-foreground`, never breaking. The garage is not in the header: it shows in the Details card under Record (AC-16). (`docs/context/vrms/design.md`, Naming a ticket.)
- **AC-12**: Issue text never shows the stored `[System] ` prefix anywhere on the page (rows, title, dialogs such as Edit steps); the category shows once, from `categoryName`. Where the page shows issue text (rows and title), a repeated root cause shows once: the text is split at its first ": " into root cause and description, and when the description starts with the root cause (case insensitive, after trimming), that repeat and any following spaces, commas, periods or colons are dropped ("Aircon not cooling: Aircon not cooling, blows warm air." reads "Aircon not cooling: blows warm air."); with nothing left only the root cause shows; with no ": ", or text that starts with ": ", the text is unchanged. The match needs a whole word: the description must equal the root cause or continue with a non letter or digit right after it (a space, comma, period or colon counts). So "Aircon: Aircon cooling weak" reads "Aircon: cooling weak", while "Air: Aircon cooling weak" stays unchanged, since "Air" is only part of the word "Aircon". Only the first ": " splits, so a root cause holding ": " is read up to it. One pure `issueDisplayText` in `lib/issueRow.ts` does it; dialogs that edit the text keep the stored text.
- **AC-13**: Photos are their own card ("Photos"), right after the Issues card and before AI, with no fold and `scroll-margin-top` for the sticky header (as the rows, AC-4). Two groups in a fixed order, Before then After, never swapping when work starts. They sit side by side (Before on the left) only when the card itself is wide enough for both rows of 4 slots: a container query on the card at 672px (`@container`, `@2xl:` in Tailwind v4), not a screen breakpoint, since the 256px app sidebar takes width. Below that they stack, Before on top. Each group sits under a small heading with its count ("Before", then "2 of 5" in muted text, split by space). Thumbnails are fixed 72px squares, at most 4 slots in a row, so the row never wraps at any width:
  - **Photos in a group**: up to 3 thumbnails. With more than 3, it shows the first 2, then a "+{N} more" tile (N = count minus 2, named "Show {N} more {before|after} photos") that opens the viewer at the group's third photo. The viewer still walks every live photo on the ticket, Before then After, as today (spec 0017, AC-24); closing it returns focus to the thumbnail or tile that opened it. Each thumbnail keeps Remove (`canRemove`) and the viewer keeps Move (`canMove`, spec 0018, AC-9).
  - **The add tile**: a 72px tile in the fourth slot of the group new photos land in, from `uploadGroup(ticket.workStartedAt != null)` (Before until work starts, After from then on, spec 0018). Its name is "Add before photo" or "Add after photo", it opens the file picker in the same click (the hidden file input sits inside the card, `aria-hidden`, driven by the tile) with no `capture` attribute, so a phone offers its camera or its gallery from the system sheet (the separate Take photo and Choose photos buttons go). The target group's tile sends no `group`, so the server files the photo as today (spec 0018). Once work has started, Before keeps a tile as well, the late Before tile, because the server allows it (AC-21) and a photo added then is marked "Added after work started" (AC-22). It names its group (`BEFORE`) in the upload. No other group has a tile. The first use of the late Before tile on a page view asks first, with `confirm()` in a new `warning` tone: a `TriangleAlert` icon in `warning-text` colour inside a round `bg-highlight/20` badge beside the title, and the confirm button keeping the default navy style, since this is a heads up and not a destructive step. The title is "Work has already started", the text is "Before photos you add now will show an "Added after work started" mark.", and the buttons are Cancel and "Add anyway". Cancel, Escape or a click outside opens no picker and returns focus to the tile. The notice belongs to the tile only: a file drop on the Before zone and a thumbnail dragged into Before skip it on purpose, since choosing that zone is already deliberate and the photo still carries the mark. "Add anyway" opens the picker, and the same page view asks no more (the answer lives in memory in `PhotosCard`, and the page is keyed by ticket id, so another ticket or a reload asks again). Some browsers (iOS Safari) may refuse a picker that opens after a dialog; if the check on a real iPhone shows that, the notice becomes a plain line under the tile and the tile opens the picker at once. The late tile's tooltip reads "Add before photo (work already started)" and its accessible name stays "Add before photo". Photos still uploading hold Start work as today (spec 0018, AC-20).
  - **Full**: when a group with a tile (the target group, and Before once work has started) has no room, counting queued uploads (`photoRoomLeft`), the tile is replaced by "{Before|After} photos full ({n} of {max})" in muted text. Picking or dropping more files than fit keeps the first ones that fit and shows today's message for the rest.
  - **Empty and read only**: a group with no photos and no tile says "None yet" (After before work starts, or either group for people who can't add; once work has started, Before shows its late tile instead). People who can't add (no `can.addFiles`, or a finished ticket) see the same groups with no tile and no limit text, and no card at all while there are no photos (spec 0017, AC-20).
  - **Drop**: there is no drop hint text at rest. While files are dragged over the card (only a drag whose `dataTransfer.types` includes `Files`; text and links show nothing), an overlay over the whole card (the `card` colour at high opacity, no new shadow) splits into two drop zones laid out like the groups (side by side when the card fits both rows, stacked otherwise), Before then After. Each zone reads "Drop for {before|after} photos" with its count ("2 of 5"), or "{Before|After} photos full ({n} of {max})" when that group has no room (a drop there adds nothing). The zone under the pointer shows the drop ring; files dropped on a zone upload to that group (AC-21), the first ones that fit as today. People who can't add photos get no drop handlers; touch screens fire no drag events. The target group's add tile has a tooltip (pointer devices) that reads "Add {before|after} photo, or drop files on this card"; the late Before tile's reads "Add before photo (work already started)".
  - **Drag between groups**: a thumbnail whose `canMove` is set can be dragged onto the other group as a shortcut for the viewer's Move (spec 0018, AC-9), with `@dnd-kit/core`: one `DndContext` in `PhotosCard` around both groups, `useDraggable` on each movable thumbnail's wrapper (never on its Remove button), `useDroppable` per group, and a `DragOverlay` showing the dragged thumbnail (so it is not clipped by the 72px row). A drop calls today's move endpoint; its errors (403 protected, 409 full or finished) show today's toasts and the photo stays. The viewer's Move button stays.

  - **Queue**: the upload queue rows sit under both groups, full width, in a polite live region, as today.
  - **Needed hint**: the card header shows "Before photo needed" or "After photo needed" (from `blockedBy` through `photoNeededHint`) in `warning-text` at its top right, only while a photo blocks the next move; a reason that is not a photo shows no hint.

Look and quality
- **AC-19**: Built to `design.md` and `docs/context/vrms/design.md` with the work order look: hierarchy from size and weight, not boxes; no icon before a label; no box inside a box; no gradient or new shadow; colour only for status, progress state, overdue, safety, the needed hint, the warning notice, Returned and destructive actions; tabular figures for counts. There are three small exceptions to "no icon before a label": the items of the `⋮` menus carry an icon, the add tiles show a plus, and the warning confirm dialog has its soft amber warning triangle beside the title. Pills (status, priority, role, and the gold Returned and Unlocked) are one shared look from `BadgePill`: sentence case, a soft tint, and a hairline ring in the pill's own text colour (`ring-1 ring-inset ring-current/20`). The tokens: the five status groups keep their `status-*-bg` and `status-*-fg` tints; High is `highlight/25` with `highlight-foreground` text; Critical is solid `destructive` with `destructive-foreground`; Medium and Low are `secondary` with `muted-strong`. Every badge built on `BadgePill` follows this, the lock and mechanic badges included. Gold pills are never solid (Returned and Unlocked use `highlight/25` too); only Critical is solid. It applies on every VRMS screen, and `docs/context/vrms/design.md` records it. No dot separator (`·`) anywhere on this page, its dialogs or the photo viewer: parts are split by space, commas, words or their own line. The wording: the summary "0 of 2 issues done, 2 of 6 steps ticked"; the account "Corporate, {corporation}"; the step tooltip "Ticked by {email} on {date}"; a Documents card row with the uploader on one line and the date on the next; the AI run line "Ran {time ago} with {model}, {confidence} confidence"; the photo viewer caption as three lines (the group, the date, the issue when tagged), since the date already holds commas; the Edit steps dialog's tag and ticked note split by space. A Vitest check fails when `·` appears in the ticket page code (`src/features/repair/` outside `repair-form/` and `RepairFormPage.tsx`). Tokens only (lint enforces it).
- **AC-20**: The page and its states (loading skeleton as 3 folded rows, empty issues, error) pass axe at 375px and 1280px, every row and control works by keyboard, and folding never moves focus. Both `⋮` menus open with Enter, Space and the arrow keys, and Escape closes them with focus back on their trigger, and that focus does not open the tooltip again (it shows again when the pointer moves over the button, or when focus leaves it and comes back). The late Before tile's warning dialog traps focus, closes with Escape as Cancel, and returns focus to the tile.

Photo groups on the server (amends spec 0018)
- **AC-21**: The photo upload (`POST /vrms/repairs/:id/attachments`, spec 0017) takes an optional multipart field `group`, `BEFORE` or `AFTER`, for photo uploads only (a document kind with `group`, or any other value, answers 400). Absent, the server picks the group from `workStartedAt` as today (spec 0018, AC-1). Present, the photo is stored as `VEHICLE_PHOTO` (Before) or `PROOF` (After), decided under the ticket row lock where today's pick runs; the same add right (`ticket.files.add`), the duplicate check across both photo kinds, and that group's cap (409 when full) apply as today, and the `ATTACHMENT_ADDED` audit row records the stored kind. Either group is allowed whatever `workStartedAt` says, matching Move (spec 0018, AC-9). `group` overrides the pick for every photo kind the upload accepts (`PHOTO`, and the older `VEHICLE_PHOTO` and `PROOF`, which read as `PHOTO`). Order of checks: the ticket's visibility and finished check stay first; then the 400 for `group` on a document or a bad value (the DTO has `@IsOptional() @IsIn(['BEFORE', 'AFTER'])`, the service rejects it on a document); then the rest of the first failing check order (spec 0017, AC-12b) runs with the group's kind as `earlyKind`, and the kind under the lock is the group's kind too. The web sends `group` for a drop on a zone and from the late Before tile (AC-13); the target group's add tile and its file picker send none.
- **AC-22**: Before photo protection (spec 0018, AC-10) follows the upload time only: a `VEHICLE_PHOTO` is protected when `tickets.protect_before_photos` is on, work has started, and its `createdAt` is at or before `workStartedAt` (inclusive; both are server times, `createdAt` set inside the upload's lock). Move never changes `createdAt`, so a photo uploaded before work started stays protected wherever it moves back to Before, and a photo uploaded after work started is never protected in Before, whether it was dropped there (AC-21) or moved there. Such a late Before photo carries a new mark `addedAfterWorkStarted` in the attachment view, shown as "Added after work started" (AC-13, Marks), to everyone who sees the photo (not gated like `takenBeforeWork`'s verifier view), so the uploader sees why Remove works and the verifier sees it is not pre-work evidence. One pure `isAddedAfterWorkStarted(row: { kind, createdAt }, workStartedAt: Date | null)` beside `isTakenBeforeWork` decides it; `isProtectedBefore` takes the row's `kind` and `createdAt` and the ticket's `workStartedAt` (replacing the `workStarted` boolean in its rights) and returns false whenever `isAddedAfterWorkStarted` is true; `canRemove` and `canMove` pass those through.

## Decision

**Chosen option**: Option 1: Foldable issue rows on the existing page, built on the data the page already has.

The Issues card becomes an accordion of issue rows with a derived state, the reference clutter goes, and the side sections move to follow the design file. The only server change is the photo group work of tasks 8 (AC-21 and AC-22).

**Implementation skills**: `shadcn` (`shadcn-ui/ui`, `.claude/skills/shadcn/`, adding the Accordion and theming it) · `vercel-react-best-practices` (`vercel-labs/agent-skills`, `.claude/skills/vercel-react-best-practices/`, the row state, effects and lists) · `tailwind-4-docs` (`.claude/skills/tailwind-4-docs/`, the fold animation and tokens)

## Consequences

**Positive**:
- A 10 issue ticket fits on one or two screens; the work in progress is open, the rest reads at a glance.
- The verifier signs off from closed rows without clicking.
- The page now follows its own design file (title, phone order, Cancel, no boxes per item).
- No new endpoint and no migration (task 8 adds one optional upload field), so little migration risk and nothing for the other groups.
- The Status card reads as a plain list of facts, and an overdue ticket is the one thing on it in colour.

**Negative / tradeoffs**:
- Done steps can't be hidden; a long, mostly done checklist stays long while its row is open (rows fold, so this costs scrolling only inside one issue).
- A Before photo added after work started is not protected, so a mechanic can remove one they put there by mistake; it is marked so a verifier knows it does not show the state before work.
- The photo card now has two drag systems: native file drops from the computer and `@dnd-kit` for moving thumbnails, each with its own feedback.
- Dropping photos is no longer advertised at rest; people find it through the add tile's tooltip or by trying.
- Activity is one more click away, though its newest entry still shows.
- Return to mechanic is outlined again next to the primary; the two choices at verification read as a pair.
- One more tap to see a closed issue's steps.
- On phones the state sits on line 2 instead of at the right edge, so the state words no longer line up down the page there (they still do from 768px). Line 2 can also wrap, so a closed row can take up to four text lines on a narrow phone (the title's two and the meta's two), though most rows need three or fewer.
- Without Expand all, reading every issue means opening each row; the verifier reads the closed rows instead, and the page remembers what was opened.
- A phone no longer has its own Take photo button; the one add tile reaches the camera through the system picker, which is one more tap on some phones.
- Typical parts and hours are only on the knowledge entry now; someone who used them as a quick parts list opens the entry.
- The "Done" state means all steps ticked, not that the mechanic says the issue is finished; an issue with no steps never shows Done.
- Per tab memory means a new tab or another device starts from the default rule.
- Verification stays per ticket; a per issue sign off (topic 3) would add an Approved state to the row later.
- The AI card stays per ticket even though it now sits next to the issues.
- The status and priority badges lose their hover labels; the row labels say what each one is.
- The pill restyle reaches every VRMS screen (lists, dashboard, Users, History, the top bar), so those screens change look with this page. The VRMS design file records it; the suite `design.md` still shows uppercase pills for the other groups.
- Copy ticket link needs the browser clipboard, which works only on https or localhost; elsewhere the toast says to copy the address from the bar.
- Someone who can add can put a Before photo in after work started with one tap and one notice. The mark keeps it honest, but the notice is the only guard.

**Neutral**:
- Feature 11's deferred tests now wait for this build.
